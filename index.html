Client Offer

Two ways to make
the room move.

Option A
The Savings Event
Clients select any piece and save 25–50% off retail. The value is instant and visible — the kind of offer that gets people in the door and keeps them buying. Works best for studios with price-conscious but quality-focused clientele.
Option B
The Free Piercing Event
Jewelry sells at full retail. The piercing is complimentary. Clients perceive exceptional value, the studio retains full jewelry margin, and your piercers earn on service too. Works best for studios with a strong reputation on the table.

Studio selects one model per event. NorVoch will advise on what's driven the most movement at comparable studios.
